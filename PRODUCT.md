# ZANED website

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React, JavaScript, Vite, and plain CSS. A runnable app that will grow into the full website.

## Product Purpose

Reproduce the supplied ZANED hero as the first part of a future production website. The reference introduces strategy, design, and technology services.

## Capabilities and Constraints

This phase includes only the header and hero. Navigation and the project CTA are enabled preview controls with hover, keyboard focus, and press feedback. Unassigned controls open nothing and keep the visitor on the hero, as requested. Supplying real URLs turns them into links. No backend, additional pages, or hosting is requested.

The shipped site also includes a silent brand introduction on every fresh page load: plain Anton “ZANED” with the existing “inc” suffix becomes all eleven supplied media assets and resolves into the supplied shaded crown. “Websites that refuse to blend in.” flips into place below the crown in Belleza and charcoal, finishing 200ms before the user-selected React Bits Morph Slider reveals the hero with a two-second melt. Escape skips; reduced motion bypasses the intro, including live preference changes.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the exact copy, self-hosted Anton headline, Belleza description and control fonts, with Space Grotesk reserved for the intro suffix, and existing TechText headline animations. Use the approved ZANED crown symbol on its own from `assets/ZANED-symbol.svg`, without wordmark lettering. The current palette follows the monochrome artwork: cool paper white, silver, graphite, and charcoal, with a softly flowing React Bits Grainient background. Use all three replacement assets together: `artwork_left.png`, `artwork_in_middle.png`, and `artwork_right.png`, preserving their original proportions, orientation, and transparency. All three artworks use the supplied React Bits GridDistortion effect with crisp grid blocks, RGB channel splitting, pointer dragging, click ripples, a settling intro, and idle motion. Reduced motion and unavailable WebGL show static originals. The hero centers the headline, supporting copy, and project CTA above a large front-facing portrait, with the outward-facing profiles staggered behind it. One camera moves through a shared 3D scene, looking toward its center while the headline and portraits remain at fixed depths. Smooth cursor tracking, a gentle idle orbit, and a slow dolly create perspective and near/far parallax. The header stays anchored; touch devices use a smaller drift, and reduced motion restores the static composition. The logo symbol sits at the left of the header, vertically centered beside the horizontally centered navigation on every screen size. It scales from 48px on desktop to 40px on tablets and 32px on phones. All screen sizes keep the centered copy above the artwork; short landscape screens place both headline phrases on one line. Keep the hero within one viewport without scrolling.

## Evidence on Hand

The navbar and CTA use the user's approved Apple-style liquid glass direction, adapted locally from React Bits Glass Surface. The centered capsule retains all three navigation labels on phones; the charcoal project control has a glass rim and a circular arrow treatment. Reduced motion removes transforms and transitions from these controls.

`reference.png` establishes the brand, typography, and copy. The user's replacement artwork in `assets/` and new layout instructions govern the artwork composition and centered navigation.
